## Day 3 - Y2K Problem

📌 Learned about the Y2K (Year 2000) Problem, a computer bug caused by storing years using only two digits (e.g., 99 instead of 1999).

### Issue
- Systems could interpret 2000 as 1900.
- This could lead to incorrect calculations and data errors.

### Impact
- Banking systems
- Government databases
- Airlines and reservation systems

### Solution
- Updated software to use four-digit years (YYYY).

💡 Key Takeaway: Small design decisions can create large-scale problems in the future, highlighting the importance of planning for scalability and maintainability.
